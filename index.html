⚙️ Grammar: Possibility and Certainty

We have used *will* and *going to* for things we are sure about. But what if we don't know the future? What if you are only 50% sure?

The Certainty Meter

50% Maybe (may, might, could)
80% - 90% Almost Sure (probably won't / will probably)
100% Very Sure (will definitely / definitely won't)

Practice: Identify the Certainty
